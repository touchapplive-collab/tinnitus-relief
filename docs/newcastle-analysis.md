# Re-analysis of the Newcastle spectral-ripple dataset (October 2026)

Source: Final_Data_Set.xlsx, doi:10.25405/data.ncl.27109693 (not redistributed here). Loader: tools/newcastle/load.py.

- 77 participants in the tracking sheet (53 ripple: amp 27, phase 26; 24 noise). Complete 6-timepoint questionnaires: 70 (48 ripple, 22 noise). 7 have no questionnaire data (5 ripple, 2 noise). Published N = 53 cannot be reproduced from the deposit; the 48 give 6.19 → 5.77 (published 6.17 → 5.70).
- Ripple n = 48, NRS loudness change over the 6-week period:
  - active: ≥1 point lower 16, ≥2 8, ≥3 4, ≥4 3; improved / same / worse 16 / 22 / 10
  - sham: ≥1 14, ≥2 3, ≥3 1, ≥4 0; improved / same / worse 14 / 16 / 18
  - active − sham −0.52 (paired p = 0.019)
- No NRS of 0 at any of 420 timepoints (all 70 people). Lowest value is 1 (4 people at one or more timepoints).
- After the 3-week washout, the change from the start of the period was −0.44 for active and +0.21 for sham (p = 0.036). Of 16 active ≥1 responders, 9 were still ≥1 lower; of 8 ≥2 responders, 5 were still ≥2 lower. No follow-up beyond the washout.
- Noise replacement (n = 22): active not better than sham.
- Predictors of active − sham difference: none significant (pitch, hearing-loss profile, duration, age, baseline, tone vs noise, effect of loud sounds). Somatosensory modulation was not collected.
